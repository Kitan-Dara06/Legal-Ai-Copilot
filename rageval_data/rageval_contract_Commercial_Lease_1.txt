Section 1.1 Subletting and Assignment
--------------------------------

The Tenant shall not sublet or assign this Lease, or any part thereof, without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Any request for consent to sublet or assign shall be made in writing and shall include all relevant information regarding the proposed sublessee or assignee, including but not limited to their financial condition, business experience, and proposed use of the Premises.

Section 1.2 Landlord Consent
-------------------------

The Landlord's consent to any subletting or assignment shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Notwithstanding the foregoing, if the proposed sublessee or assignee has a net worth of $10,000,000 or more, the Landlord's consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, and any withholding of consent shall be deemed unreasonable unless the Landlord can demonstrate that the proposed sublessee or assignee is not a reputable and financially responsible party. The Landlord shall respond to any request for consent within ten (10) business days of receipt of the request, and failure to respond within such time period shall be deemed to be an unreasonable withholding of consent.

Section 1.3 Conditions for Consent
---------------------------------

The Landlord may impose reasonable conditions on any consent to sublet or assign, including but not limited to:

* The proposed sublessee or assignee providing financial statements and other information demonstrating their financial responsibility;
* The proposed sublessee or assignee executing a guaranty of the Lease;
* The proposed sublessee or assignee providing proof of insurance;
* The proposed sublessee or assignee complying with all applicable laws and regulations.

Section 1.4 Assignment
------------------

Any assignment of this Lease shall be subject to the following conditions:

* The assignee shall assume all of the obligations of the Tenant under this Lease;
* The assignee shall provide the Landlord with written notice of the assignment, including the name and address of the assignee;
* The assignee shall be bound by all of the terms and conditions of this Lease, including but not limited to the payment of rent and the observance of all covenants and conditions.

Section 1.5 Subletting
------------------

Any subletting of the Premises shall be subject to the following conditions:

* The sublessee shall be bound by all of the terms and conditions of this Lease, including but not limited to the payment of rent and the observance of all covenants and conditions;
* The sublessee shall provide the Landlord with written notice of the subletting, including the name and address of the sublessee;
* The Tenant shall remain liable for all obligations under this Lease, including but not limited to the payment of rent and the observance of all covenants and conditions.

Section 1.6 Transfer Fees
---------------------

The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord a transfer fee in the amount of $5,000 in the event of any assignment or subletting of the Premises. The transfer fee shall be payable within ten (10) business days of the effective date of the assignment or subletting.

Section 1.7 Defaults
-----------------

Any default by the assignee or sublessee shall be deemed to be a default by the Tenant under this Lease, and the Landlord may enforce all of its rights and remedies against the Tenant. The Tenant shall remain liable for all obligations under this Lease, including but not limited to the payment of rent and the observance of all covenants and conditions.

Section 1.9 Entire Agreement
-------------------------

This Lease constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings, and agreements between the parties. This Lease may not be amended or modified except in writing signed by both parties. 

Section 1.10 Counterparts
-----------------------

This Lease may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed to be an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. 

Section 1.11 Notice
----------------

Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Lease shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been duly given if delivered personally, sent by certified mail, or sent by facsimile transmission. 

Section 1.12 Severability
----------------------

If any provision of this Lease is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. 

Section 1.13 Binding Effect
-------------------------

This Lease shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and assigns. 

Section 1.14 Definitions
----------------------

For the purposes of this Lease, the following definitions shall apply:

* "Premises" means the leased premises, including all improvements and fixtures.
* "Rent" means the monthly rent payable by the Tenant to the Landlord.
* "Term" means the term of this Lease, commencing on the commencement date and ending on the expiration date.
