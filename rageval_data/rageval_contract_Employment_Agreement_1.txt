Section 1.1 Termination Notice
The Employer and the Employee agree that, in the event of termination of this Agreement by either party, a written notice of termination shall be provided to the other party. The notice period shall be strictly 90 days, and the Employee shall continue to perform their duties and receive their salary and benefits during this notice period.

Section 1.2 Severance
In the event of termination of this Agreement by the Employer, other than for cause, the Employer shall provide the Employee with severance pay equivalent to six (6) months of the Employee's base salary, paid in accordance with the Employer's regular pay schedule. The severance pay shall be paid in installments over the six (6) month period, and shall be subject to all applicable taxes and deductions.

Section 1.3 Mitigation of Damages
The Employee shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate the damages resulting from the termination of this Agreement, including but not limited to, actively seeking new employment. The Employee shall provide the Employer with written notification of any new employment or other income-earning activities within 14 days of commencing such activities. The Employer's obligation to provide severance pay shall be reduced by the amount of any income earned by the Employee during the severance period, and the Employee shall reimburse the Employer for any overpayment of severance pay.

Section 1.4 Termination for Cause
Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 1.1 and Section 1.2, the Employer may terminate this Agreement immediately, without notice or severance pay, in the event of termination for cause. For the purposes of this Agreement, "cause" shall mean: (i) the Employee's material breach of this Agreement; (ii) the Employee's gross negligence or willful misconduct; (iii) the Employee's conviction of a crime; or (iv) the Employee's failure to perform their duties and responsibilities as reasonably directed by the Employer.

Section 1.5 Notice of Termination
Any notice of termination required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing, and shall be delivered personally or sent by registered mail, return receipt requested, to the address of the other party as set forth in this Agreement or to such other address as either party may hereafter specify in writing. Notice shall be deemed to have been given: (i) on the date of personal delivery; or (ii) on the third business day after the date of mailing.

Section 1.6 Release
As a condition of receiving severance pay, the Employee shall execute a release in favor of the Employer, in a form satisfactory to the Employer, releasing the Employer from any and all claims, demands, and causes of action, whether known or unknown, arising from or related to the Employee's employment with the Employer or the termination of this Agreement.

Section 1.7 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [STATE/PROVINCE]. Any disputes or claims arising from or related to this Agreement shall be resolved through [DISPUTE RESOLUTION PROCESS], in accordance with the laws of [STATE/PROVINCE].

Section 1.8 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof, and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings, and agreements between the parties. This Agreement may not be amended or modified except in writing signed by both parties.

Section 1.9 Survival
The provisions of Section 1.2 (Severance), Section 1.3 (Mitigation of Damages), and Section 1.6 (Release) shall survive the termination of this Agreement, and shall remain in full force and effect notwithstanding the termination of this Agreement.
