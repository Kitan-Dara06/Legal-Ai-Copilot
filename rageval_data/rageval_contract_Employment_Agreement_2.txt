Section 1.1: Termination Notice

The Employee's employment with the Company may be terminated by either party upon ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the other. The notice period shall commence on the date the written notice is received by the other party. During the notice period, the Employee shall continue to perform their duties and responsibilities to the best of their abilities and shall cooperate with the Company to ensure a smooth transition of their responsibilities.

Section 1.2: Severance

In the event that the Employee's employment is terminated by the Company without cause, or if the Employee terminates their employment with the Company for good reason, the Company shall provide the Employee with severance pay equivalent to six (6) months of the Employee's base salary, paid in accordance with the Company's regular payroll schedule. The severance pay shall be paid in installments over the six (6) month period following the date of termination, provided that the Employee has complied with the terms of this Agreement, including the mitigation of damages provision set forth in Section 1.3 below.

Section 1.3: Mitigation of Damages

The Employee shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate the damages resulting from the termination of their employment by actively seeking new employment and providing the Company with written notice of any new employment or other arrangements that may affect their entitlement to severance pay. The Employee's failure to mitigate damages shall result in a reduction of the severance pay payable under Section 1.2, and the Company shall be entitled to offset any amounts earned by the Employee during the severance period against the severance pay.

Section 1.4: Conditions Precedent to Severance

The Employee's entitlement to severance pay under Section 1.2 is conditional upon the Employee: (i) executing a release of claims in favor of the Company and its affiliates, in a form satisfactory to the Company; (ii) returning all Company property, including but not limited to documents, equipment, and other materials; and (iii) complying with the terms of this Agreement, including the confidentiality and non-solicitation provisions. The Company shall provide the Employee with the release of claims document within ten (10) days of the date of termination, and the Employee shall execute and return the release to the Company within twenty-one (21) days of receipt.

Section 1.5: Termination for Cause

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 1.1, the Company may terminate the Employee's employment immediately without notice or severance pay if the Employee: (i) is convicted of a felony or a crime involving moral turpitude; (ii) engages in willful misconduct or gross negligence that causes harm to the Company; (iii) breaches any material term of this Agreement; or (iv) fails to comply with any lawful directive of the Company. In the event of termination for cause, the Employee shall not be entitled to any severance pay or other benefits under this Agreement.

Section 1.6: Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following definitions shall apply: "base salary" means the Employee's annual salary in effect at the time of termination, excluding any bonuses, commissions, or other forms of compensation; "cause" means the events described in Section 1.5; "good reason" means the Employee's termination of employment due to a material reduction in their duties or responsibilities, a reduction in their base salary, or a change in their reporting relationship or work location; and "severance period" means the six (6) month period following the date of termination.

Section 1.7: Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the state in which the Company is headquartered. Any disputes arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved through arbitration in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association, and the arbitration shall be conducted in the city where the Company is headquartered. The parties hereby waive any right to a trial by jury and agree to be bound by the arbitrator's decision.
