## Section 1.1: Subletting and Assignment

The Tenant shall not sublet or assign this Lease, or any interest herein, without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Any attempt to sublet or assign without such consent shall be void and of no effect.

## Section 1.2: Landlord's Consent

The Landlord's consent to any subletting or assignment shall not be unreasonably withheld, provided that the proposed sublessee or assignee has a net worth of at least $10,000,000. In determining whether to grant consent, the Landlord shall consider the creditworthiness, financial stability, and business reputation of the proposed sublessee or assignee. The Landlord shall respond to any request for consent within thirty (30) days of receipt of such request, and failure to respond within such timeframe shall be deemed a reasonable withholding of consent.

## Section 1.3: Conditions for Consent

The Landlord may impose reasonable conditions on any consent to sublet or assign, including but not limited to: (i) the payment of a fee to compensate the Landlord for its expenses in reviewing the proposed sublessee or assignee; (ii) the provision of additional security or guarantees; and (iii) the execution of a new lease or amendment to this Lease on terms acceptable to the Landlord. The Tenant shall be responsible for paying any fees or expenses incurred by the Landlord in connection with the review and processing of any request for consent.

## Section 1.4: Assignment of Lease

In the event of an assignment of this Lease, the assignee shall assume all of the obligations and liabilities of the Tenant under this Lease, and the Tenant shall remain liable for any breaches of this Lease occurring prior to the effective date of the assignment. The assignee shall provide the Landlord with written notice of the assignment, including the assignee's name, address, and contact information, within ten (10) days of the effective date of the assignment.

## Section 1.5: Subletting

In the event of a subletting, the sublessee shall be bound by all of the terms and conditions of this Lease, and the Tenant shall remain liable for any breaches of this Lease occurring during the term of the sublease. The Tenant shall provide the Landlord with written notice of the subletting, including the sublessee's name, address, and contact information, within ten (10) days of the commencement of the sublease.

## Section 1.6: No Release of Tenant

Any subletting or assignment shall not release the Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, and the Tenant shall remain liable for the payment of rent and all other obligations under this Lease. The Tenant shall be responsible for ensuring that any sublessee or assignee complies with all terms and conditions of this Lease, and shall be liable for any damages or losses resulting from any breach of this Lease by the sublessee or assignee.

## Section 1.7: Landlord's Rights

The Landlord reserves the right to terminate this Lease in the event of any assignment or subletting that is not in compliance with the terms of this Section 1. The Landlord may also terminate this Lease if the Tenant or any sublessee or assignee fails to comply with any term or condition of this Lease. Upon termination, the Tenant shall vacate the Premises and surrender possession to the Landlord, and shall be liable for any damages or losses resulting from the termination.

## Section 1.8: Governing Law

This Section 1 shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the state in which the Premises are located. Any disputes arising out of or related to this Section 1 shall be resolved through arbitration in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association, and the arbitrator's decision shall be final and binding on the parties.

## Section 1.9: Entire Agreement

This Section 1 constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subletting and assignment of this Lease, and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings, and agreements between the parties. This Section 1 may not be amended or modified except in writing signed by both parties.

## Section 1.10: Notice

Any notice or request required or permitted to be given under this Section 1 shall be in writing and shall be delivered to the other party by certified mail, return receipt requested, or by overnight courier. Notice shall be deemed to have been given on the date of receipt by the other party.
